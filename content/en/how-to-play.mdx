## What Machine Party is

Machine Party is a violent online party game for two to four players. Every simulation changes the objective, so the safest habit is to read the instruction in front of you instead of carrying assumptions from the previous round.

Bring your group before starting. Online co-op is supported, but the base game does not include a public matchmaking list.

## Standard play or Arcade mode?

Standard play keeps the opening and ending presentation around the simulations. Arcade mode removes that framing and gives the group a shorter randomized run.

## Three things to do before each round

Read the complete briefing. Identify your character or subject. Then find the hazard, boundary, timer, or other condition that can end the attempt immediately.

## 10 beginner tips

### 1. Read the full round briefing

The goal, danger, and scoring rule may appear separately. Acting on the first phrase you notice is a common avoidable mistake.

### 2. Identify your subject before moving

Confirm what belongs to you and where it starts. This keeps you from following another player's movement.

### 3. Find the failure condition first

Once you can name the urgent threat, make a first move that leaves room to adjust.

### 4. Compress the rule into one callout

Turn the briefing into a short phrase the group can repeat: “stay clear,” “carry this,” or “follow the target.”

### 5. Handle one input at a time

After an error, make one deliberate correction. Panic inputs hide what changed.

### 6. Leave adjustment space

Spacing makes every player's movement easier to read and keeps escape routes open.

### 7. Watch the hazard and an exit

Do not stare only at the threat. Keep a safe destination in mind.

### 8. Interfere with a purpose

Secure your own position before disrupting somebody else, and only do it when it helps the current objective.

### 9. Scale risk to the score

Protect a useful result when you can. Take the bold option only when the score actually demands it.

### 10. Keep watching after elimination

An eliminated player can still learn the hazard pattern and report useful information for the next attempt.

## Common beginner mistakes

- Skipping instructions because the last round looked similar.
- Mashing inputs after one error.
- Crowding one route when the team can spread out.
- Watching the hazard without planning an exit.
- Abandoning the objective to attack another player.

## Beginner FAQ

### How many people can play?

Machine Party supports two to four players.

### Does every minigame use the same strategy?

No. Read the briefing every time and treat earlier rounds as experience, not a fixed rulebook.

### Is Arcade mode a shortcut?

It is a shorter random format, but every round still requires the same attention to instructions.
